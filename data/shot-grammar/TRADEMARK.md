# H.SYN.EMA Trademark Policy

The format is free. The **name** is not, and that asymmetry is deliberate: it is the whole
commercial architecture in one sentence.

`PATENTS.md` grants every patent right needed to implement, irrevocably and at no charge.
This document reserves the marks — **H.SYN.EMA**, **Westwyrd Shot Grammar**, and the
conformance mark **H.SYN.EMA Verified** — because a conformance mark that anyone may apply
to themselves is worth nothing to the party relying on it.

---

## What anyone may do, without asking

- Implement the specification, completely, commercially, forever.
- **State factually** that a product reads, writes or verifies H.SYN.EMA files. *"Supports
  H.SYN.EMA"* and *"implements the H.SYN.EMA specification"* are statements of fact and
  need no permission.
- Reproduce and redistribute the specification, the vocabularies and the conformance
  vectors, under CC BY 4.0.
- Run the public conformance vectors against their own implementation and publish the
  results, including failures, including ours.

## What requires certification

- Using **H.SYN.EMA Verified**, the conformance mark, or any confusingly similar mark.
- Naming a product such that it reads as an official implementation.
- Stating or implying that an implementation is *certified*, *compliant*, or *approved*.

Certification means the public conformance suite was run against the implementation **by a
disinterested party** and passed. That is the only thing the mark asserts, and it is the
only thing anyone is paying for.

## Why the vectors are public but the certificate is not

Both halves are load-bearing.

**The vectors are public** so that anyone can prove their own implementation correct without
asking permission, without a fee, and without a relationship. A conformance suite behind a
paywall is a tollbooth, and tollbooths get forked — see `PATENTS.md` §3.

**The certificate is issued** because a studio putting a delivery requirement into a
contract needs somebody other than the vendor to have checked. Self-assertion cannot supply
that, no matter how good the vectors are. The value is not the test; it is the
disinterestedness.

## The mark is the business

Stated plainly so nobody has to infer it. When a distributor writes *H.SYN.EMA Verified*
into a delivery specification, every vendor in that chain needs the mark — and **the vendors
pay, not the studio**. The format being free is what allows the requirement to be written
at all; the mark being controlled is what makes the requirement mean something.

Neither half works alone.

## Enforcement posture

Against misuse of the conformance mark: firm, because a false certification harms the party
who relied on it more than it harms us.

Against everything else: permissive. Factual reference, comparison, criticism, academic use
and unflattering benchmark results are all fine and none of them require a conversation.
A trademark policy used to suppress a bad review is a trademark policy that has stopped
protecting anyone.

## Status

Marks are asserted as unregistered at time of publication. Registration is intended in the
jurisdictions where the conformance programme operates. Nothing in this document is a
representation that registration has issued.
