# H.SYN.EMA Patent Grant

**Version 1.0.** Applies to the H.SYN.EMA format specification, the H.SYN.EMA codec design,
the Westwyrd Shot Grammar, and the reference implementations published alongside them
(together, the **Specification**).

---

## 1 · The grant

Westwyrd Federated Instruments grants to every person and entity a **perpetual, worldwide,
non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free, irrevocable** licence under any Necessary Claims it
owns or controls, to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale, import, and otherwise
distribute any Implementation of the Specification.

**Necessary Claims** are claims of a patent that are necessarily infringed by implementing
the Specification, where there is no commercially reasonable non-infringing alternative.

**Implementation** means any product, service, or portion of one that conforms to the
Specification.

No fee, no reporting, no registration, and no notice is required to exercise this grant.
Nothing has to be signed. Nothing has to be asked.

## 2 · Defensive termination

This grant terminates automatically, as to a party, if that party initiates or voluntarily
joins a patent infringement claim against any other party alleging that an Implementation
of the Specification infringes a patent.

It terminates only for the asserting party, only prospectively, and only for the duration of
the assertion. Every other implementer is unaffected.

This clause is the only condition on the grant, and it exists so that the grant survives.

## 3 · Why the grant is irrevocable, stated plainly

A format that can be taxed is a format that will be routed around. HEVC fragmented across
four licensors, implementers backed away rather than accept unbounded royalty exposure, and
a royalty-free alternative took the market inside a few years.

This grant is therefore not a concession made under pressure and cannot be withdrawn when
adoption arrives. **The commercial position around this format is the registry, the
conformance mark, the verification service, and support — none of which exist unless the
format itself is free.** Making that irreversible is the point.

## 4 · Why claims are filed at all

Filing and then granting away is not a contradiction.

Unfiled claims leave the ecosystem open to a third party patenting around a published
specification and taxing everyone who adopted it in good faith. Filed and granted
royalty-free, the same claims make the format permanently safe to adopt and permanently
unsafe to attack.

The filing is the fence. The grant is the invitation.

## 5 · What this grant does not do

- It does not license trademarks. **H.SYN.EMA**, the conformance mark, and related names
  are governed separately — see `TRADEMARK.md`. An implementation may conform; only a
  certified implementation may say so in a name or a logo.
- It does not license patents held by third parties, and no representation is made that
  none exist.
- It does not grant rights in any service, registry, test harness, or hosted verification
  operated by Westwyrd Federated Instruments.
- It does not create a warranty. The Specification is provided as-is.

## 6 · Scope

This grant covers the Specification as published, and each future revision as published.
A revision cannot narrow the grant made for a prior revision. An implementation of
revision N keeps its licence forever, whatever revision N+1 says.
