## Third-party material

The industry taxonomy, the notation grammars and the studio registry mirror under
`taxonomy/`, `notation/` and `registries/` are assembled from public sources, each row
carrying a `source_key` resolving against `_schema/sources.csv`. Those sources retain their
own terms; the assembly, the schema and the annotations are ours under CC BY 4.0.

The ISDCF studio code registry is reproduced as published, as a mirror, and remains ISDCF's.
The ETC/MovieLabs VFX Image Sequence Naming specification is CC BY 3.0 and is cited, not
reproduced.
